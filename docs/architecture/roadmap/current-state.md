## Current State

As of 2026-09-19 Hopin is a **reference architecture case study**, not a product
in development. Nothing is built, deployed or measured. The only code is a thin
running slice for evidence, [slice/](../../../slice/README.md) (plan step S123),
measured locally on 2026-09-19 ([evidence](../evidence/s123-slice-results.md)).

What exists:

- The master plan, [docs/hopin-plan.md](../../hopin-plan.md), with steps S001–S127.
- This architecture knowledge base: target model, ADRs 1–8, constraints,
  requirements and concern documents.
- The S002 regulatory memo, [docs/compliance/s002-regulatory-memo.md](../../compliance/s002-regulatory-memo.md).
- A business case: white-label dispatch platform first, Hopin brand second
  ([business case](../../business/business-case.md)).
- A public GitHub repository with a protected `main` branch.

What does not exist yet: the full application, infrastructure, cloud accounts, a
legal entity, a dispatch partner or licence, and any measurement against the
quality attributes.
